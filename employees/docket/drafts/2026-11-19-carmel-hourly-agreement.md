## 7. Placement fees and outside agencies

7.1 **Placement fee.** When Carmel or an affiliate hires an Eden Candidate, Carmel pays a placement fee of **20% of First-Year Base Salary**, invoiced on Start and due 30 days after Start. This applies during the Hourly Period and after it. Hourly fees are not credited against placement fees.

7.2 **Carmel Candidates.** No placement fee is due on a Carmel Candidate. Eden's work on Carmel Candidates is paid by the hour.

7.3 **After the Hourly Period.** Fees after Day 30 follow the terms chosen at the Day 30 Review, or this Section 7 under 11.4.

7.4 **Outside-agency placements** through Eden cost no more than **24% of First-Year Base Salary** in all (Section 7.6), invoiced on Start and due 30 days after Start.

7.5 **Carmel approves; Eden runs a one-time split.** Eden manages an outside agency only on a role Carmel approves in writing, under a one-time split agreement between Eden and that agency. The agency submits through Eden, Eden invoices Carmel and Eden pays the agency. Carmel has no contract with that agency for that placement and pays it nothing.

7.6 **Fee cap.** Carmel pays no more than 24% of First-Year Base Salary in all for an outside-agency placement. Eden's management fee of 6 points is inside the cap, and each invoice shows it. Outside-agency placements carry the guarantee in Section 8, and Eden's split agreement passes the replacement duty through to the agency.

7.7 **Duplicates.** The first Submittal Carmel receives wins.

7.8 **Carmel's existing agency agreements** stay Carmel's. Carmel may tell those agencies that from 1 January 2027 it pays no more than 24% and routes new agency submittals through Eden. Carmel decides and sends any such notice; Eden does not contact Carmel's agencies about their terms unless Carmel asks it to.

## 8. Replacement guarantee

8.1 **120 days.** If an Eden Candidate hire, or an outside-agency placement under Section 7.5, leaves, or Carmel lets them go for performance or conduct, within 120 days of Start, Eden runs one replacement search at no added fee.

8.2 **Conditions.** Carmel tells Eden within 10 Business Days of the departure, and the fee was paid by its due date.

8.3 **Exclusions.** The guarantee covers Eden Candidates and outside-agency placements only, not Carmel Candidates and not time billed hourly. It does not apply if the role is eliminated, the hire is laid off in a restructuring, or Carmel materially changes the role, pay or location. During a replacement search, Eden does not bill hourly for that role unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.

9.3 **In-Process Candidates.** Candidates already in Carmel's process at the Start Date are listed in Schedule 2, with whether any fee applies to each. Candidates Eden submitted before the Start Date stay under the terms they were submitted on.

10.4 **Automated tools.** Eden does not use an automated tool to make or substantially assist a hiring decision on a Carmel role without Carmel's written approval. Eden's Submittal is a human assessment.

10.5 **Confidential information.** Each Party keeps the other's non-public business information confidential and uses it only for this Agreement, during its term and for two (2) years after.
