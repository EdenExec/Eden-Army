# Carmel Partners & Eden Partner Group

### Recruiting Services Agreement

This agreement is between Carmel Partners and its affiliates, 1000 Sansome Street, First Floor, San Francisco, CA 94111 ("**Carmel**"), and Eden Partner Group LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company, 4611 NW Fruit Valley Road, Vancouver, WA 98660 ("**Eden**"). Eden will work Carmel's open searches as part of its team for 30 days, billed by the hour, and the parties will then decide together how to continue.

## 1. Term and hourly fees

1.1 **Term.** This agreement starts on [Monday 23 November 2026] (the "**Start Date**") and runs for 30 days, then continues under Section 5.

1.2 **Rate.** Carmel pays **$220 an hour** for time anyone on the Eden team spends on Carmel's searches in Schedule 1 or roles Carmel later assigns by email (the "**Covered Roles**").

1.3 **Not-to-exceed.** Hourly fees for each 30-day period will not exceed **$19,800** without Carmel's written approval (email is enough). Eden keeps detailed time records and shares them on request.

1.4 **Invoices.** Eden invoices weekly. Each invoice is due 15 days after its date. Hourly fees are not refunded or credited against placement fees.

1.5 **Ending early.** Either party may end this agreement on 10 business days' written notice. Carmel pays for time worked to the end date. Sections 2, 3, 6 and 7 survive.

## 2. Placement fee and guarantee

2.1 **Fee.** If Carmel or an affiliate hires an Eden Candidate, for any role, as an employee or contractor, within 12 months of Eden first submitting that candidate, Carmel pays **20% of first-year base salary** (excluding bonus, sign-on, relocation, benefits and equity), invoiced on the hire's start date and due 30 days later.

2.2 **Eden Candidate.** A candidate Eden submits, with their consent, who is not a Carmel applicant or employee referral and who did not, in the 6 months before Eden's submittal, interview with Carmel or reach Carmel through another agency. No fee is due on Carmel's own applicants or referrals; the hourly rate covers Eden's work on them.

2.3 **Candidates already in process.** Candidates in Carmel's process at the Start Date, and their fee status, are confirmed by email at each role's hand-off. Candidates Eden submitted before the Start Date keep their original terms.

2.4 **Guarantee.** If a placed hire leaves or is let go for performance or conduct within 120 days of starting, and the fee was paid on time, Eden runs one replacement search with no added fee and no hourly billing, unless the role was eliminated, the hire was laid off, or Carmel materially changed the role, pay or location.

## 3. Outside agencies and Carmel's agency notice

3.1 **Non-exclusive.** Carmel may keep recruiting directly and may use other agencies. If Carmel approves an outside agency working through Eden on a role, Carmel pays no more than **24% of first-year base salary** in all, Eden's management fee included, and Eden pays the agency.

3.2 **Agency notice.** This agreement overrides Carmel's standard agency notice and terms for Eden's work, and Carmel keeps Eden's submittals inside Carmel and uses them only to consider the candidate for Carmel roles.

## 4. Data and confidentiality

Carmel's applicant data stays in Carmel's systems. Eden uses it only for this work, does not copy it into Eden's systems, and deletes or returns it within 10 business days after this agreement ends. Eden does not use an automated tool to make or substantially assist a hiring decision without Carmel's written approval; every submittal is a human assessment. Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential during this agreement and for two years after.

## 5. Day 30

On or about [22 December 2026] the parties review the month's body of work together (where each search started and stands, the candidates screened and presented, and the process built) and agree in writing how to continue. If no decision is made, these hourly terms continue month to month until either party ends them under Section 1.5.

## 6. Legal terms

Eden is an independent contractor and pays its own people. Each party will defend and indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from its own breach of this agreement, negligence or wilful misconduct. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages or lost profits, and each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid to Eden in the 12 months before the claim; neither limit applies to unpaid fees, indemnity, breach of Section 4, or fraud or wilful misconduct. Eden carries general, professional and employment practices liability insurance and provides certificates on request. Washington law governs, and the state and federal courts in Clark County, Washington have exclusive jurisdiction. This is the entire agreement on its subject, replaces all earlier drafts, controls over any Carmel standard terms, and changes only in a writing signed by both parties. It may be signed in counterparts and electronically.

## 7. Interim terms

If Carmel accepts this agreement by email before signing, both parties are bound by it from the Start Date until it is signed.
