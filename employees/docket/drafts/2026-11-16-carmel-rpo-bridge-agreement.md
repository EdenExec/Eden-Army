### Background

Carmel is restructuring its HR function and needs recruiting capacity on its open requisitions while it does. Eden has placed candidates with Carmel since 2018. The Parties want a short, non-exclusive, retained bridge: Eden works Carmel's open requisitions as Carmel's recruiting team for 90 days, the retainer is credited in full against hire fees, and at the end the Parties decide whether to continue.

## 2. Term

2.1 **Ninety days.** This Agreement runs from the Start Date to the End Date.

2.2 **Lifted Weeks.** The Lifted Weeks do not count toward the Term or the service standards, and no retainer covers them. Eden still keeps live offers and scheduled interviews moving in those weeks.

2.3 **Ending early.** Either Party may end this Agreement at any time on 30 days' written notice. Retainer already paid is not refunded, but it stays available as credit under Section 6.

2.4 **After the End Date.** This Agreement ends on the End Date unless the Parties agree in writing to extend it, either month to month on these terms or as a longer RPO agreement. 2.5 **Interim terms.** If Carmel accepts these terms by email before signing, both Parties are bound by them from the Start Date until this Agreement is signed.

## 3. Non-exclusive

3.1 Eden's engagement is **not exclusive**. Carmel may keep recruiting directly, use its own employee referrals and use other agencies on any role, Covered Roles included.

3.2 Eden may work for other clients, but does not recruit Carmel employees for any other client while this Agreement is in force (Section 12).

## 4. Services

4.1 **In scope.**

| Eden does | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| Recruiting on Covered Roles, including sourcing once each role's hand-off is done | Employer brand and Carmel's LinkedIn page |
| Hiring-manager management: intake, calibration and feedback | Job posting and UKG administration |
| Screening Eden-sourced candidates, Carmel applicants and employee referrals | Onboarding |
| Submittals on Eden's standard format | Compensation consulting and formal benchmarking |
| Interview coordination through to offer stage | HR generalist work |
| Informal compensation checks from Eden's market conversations | Offers, background checks and hiring decisions, which stay with Carmel |

4.2 **Compensation checks are informal.** They are a read of what candidates in the market tell Eden. They are not a benchmark or advice. Eden will not ask candidates about salary history where the law bars it.

4.3 **Offer stage.** Carmel makes and extends every offer. Eden keeps the candidate warm and reports what the candidate expects.

4.4 **Changes.** A new service is added only in writing, with its price agreed first.

## 5. What Carmel provides

5.1 **Applicants.** Resumes from UKG for each Covered Role: a spreadsheet of linked resumes plus a shared folder Carmel controls, refreshed at least twice a week.

5.2 **Access.** A LinkedIn Recruiter seat in an Eden user's own name under Carmel's licence, if Carmel's licence allows it; UKG user IDs where needed; and access to hiring managers.

5.3 **Decisions.** A named Carmel lead with authority to assign roles and approve terms (Brandy Fulton), and a named delegate when she is away. Hiring-manager feedback within 2 business days of a Submittal, and standing weekly interview availability.

5.4 **Handover.** Seven half-hour hiring-manager hand-offs in the first week, and Schedule 2 (In-Process Candidates) agreed by both Parties before Eden contacts anyone on it.

## 7. Outside agencies

7.1 **Carmel approves, Eden runs a one-time split.** Eden manages an outside agency only on a role Carmel approves in writing, under a one-time split agreement between Eden and that agency. The agency submits through Eden, Eden invoices Carmel and Eden pays the agency. Carmel has no contract with that agency for that placement and pays it nothing.

7.2 **Fee cap.** Carmel pays no more than 20% of First-Year Base Salary in all for a third-party placement. Eden's management fee of [__] points is inside the cap, and each invoice shows it. [Kev: set the points.] Third-party placements carry the same guarantee as Eden's (Section 8).

7.3 **Duplicates.** The first Submittal Carmel receives wins.

7.4 **Carmel's existing agency agreements** stay Carmel's. As an option, Carmel may tell those agencies that from 1 January 2027 it pays no more than 20% and routes new agency submittals through Eden. Carmel decides and sends any such notice; Eden does not contact Carmel's agencies about their terms.

## 8. Replacement guarantee

8.1 **120 days.** If a hire leaves, or Carmel lets them go for performance or conduct, within 120 days of Start, Eden runs one replacement search at no added fee and no new retainer.

8.2 **Conditions.** Carmel tells Eden within 10 business days of the departure, and the fee was paid by its due date.

8.3 **Exclusions.** The guarantee does not apply if the role is eliminated, the hire is laid off in a restructuring, or Carmel materially changes the role, pay or location. Credit already applied to the first invoice stays applied.

## 9. Candidate ownership

9.1 **Twelve months.** If Carmel or an affiliate hires an Eden Candidate, for any role, as an employee or contractor, within 12 months of Eden's first Submittal of that candidate, the fee in Section 6 is due. This survives the end of this Agreement.

9.2 **Already in Carmel's process.** A candidate is not Eden's if, in the 6 months before Eden's Submittal, they interviewed with Carmel or another agency submitted them to Carmel. Carmel tells Eden so, with the record, within 2 business days of the Submittal; otherwise the candidate is Eden's.

9.3 **In-Process Candidates.** Candidates already in Carmel's process at the Start Date are listed in Schedule 2 with the fee, if any, that applies to each. Candidates Eden submitted before the Start Date stay under the terms they were submitted on.

9.4 **Submittals stay inside Carmel.** Carmel keeps Eden's Submittals within Carmel and does not forward them outside it.

## 10. Data and confidentiality

10.1 **Carmel's data stays with Carmel.** Applicant data stays in Carmel's systems and the shared folder. Eden uses it only for this work, does not copy it into Eden's systems, and does not sell or share it. Eden will sign Carmel's standard privacy (CCPA service-provider) terms.

10.2 **Named people only.** Each Eden person with access is named to Carmel and bound to confidentiality in writing. Eden answers for them. Eden tells Carmel within 72 hours of discovering any unauthorised access to Carmel data.

10.3 **At the end.** Within 10 business days after this Agreement ends, Eden deletes or returns Carmel data (Carmel chooses), gives up its access and confirms in writing.

10.4 **Automated tools.** Eden does not use an automated tool to make or substantially assist a hiring decision on a Carmel role without Carmel's written approval. Eden's submittal is a human assessment.

10.5 **Confidential information.** Each Party keeps the other's non-public business information confidential and uses it only for this Agreement, during the Term and for [2] years after. [Docket: standard carve-outs (public, already known, independently developed, required by law).]
