## Verdict: SIGN WITH THESE FIXES

The deal is intact: hourly fees, 20%, month to month, no 6% fee, and Carmel makes the offers. But Carmel's standard terms removed four of our protections and added one unseen document. Send Brandy items 1 to 5 now, as small asks, before her legal and IT teams finalise. Your "no issues at surface level" reply still holds: the fixes are about plumbing, not price. Items 6 to 12 are optional; trade them away if needed.

- **Must fix (1 to 5):** no liability cap; unseen AI and cyber policies; unpaid notice period; earlier submittals wiped out; a weaker test for who counts as an Eden candidate.
- **Better for Eden than our draft:** the $19,800 not-to-exceed is gone, the insurance duty is vaguer, and Carmel warrants the information it gives us.
- **One settled term is missing:** "applicant data stays in Carmel's systems". Carmel's IT team will probably add it back; we should hold ourselves to it either way (see item 2).
