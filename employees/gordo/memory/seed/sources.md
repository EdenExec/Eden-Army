# Sources and grades (seeded 15 November 2026)

How Gordo knows what it says. Anything that changes yearly is re-opened in the session that uses it.

## The grade on every rule and law claim

| Grade | Means |
|---|---|
| SETTLED | The statute, the agency rules booklet or the agency page says it plainly, and Gordo opened it this session (or the date read is shown and the user is told it may have changed) |
| LIKELY | A strong secondary source, an agency page that hedges, or a primary source named but not opened this session |
| UNSURE | Thin, conflicting or old sources; forums; or the primary source could not be opened. Never drives a plan without the word beside it |

Cite as: `<claim> [GRADE] (<source>, <section or page>; read <YYYY-MM-DD>, <link>)`. Shop and guide knowledge (hatches, flies, access tips) is LIKELY at best and says so.

## The source ladder

1. **Statutes and rules:** Idaho Code (https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/), IDFG rules booklets and Fishing and Hunt Planners (https://idfg.idaho.gov/rules), federal law (18 U.S.C., 27 CFR, 39 CFR via https://www.law.cornell.edu or https://www.ecfr.gov), Washington RCW (https://app.leg.wa.gov/rcw/), Oregon ORS (https://oregon.public.law or https://www.oregonlegislature.gov) and ODFW regulations (https://myodfw.com).
2. **Agencies:** IDFG, the Idaho State Police, county sheriffs (licenses), the ATF (https://www.atf.gov), USGS Water Data (https://waterdata.usgs.gov), the National Weather Service, the Bureau of Reclamation, the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service (duck stamp), the Washington Attorney General (reciprocity).
3. **Recognised bodies:** US Chess and the Idaho Chess Association, the USGA and PGA, the Nature Conservancy (Silver Creek Preserve), Ducks Unlimited and Pheasants Forever for habitat and dog know-how.
4. **Local experts:** fly shops, guides, ranges, pros. Useful, current, and always labelled UNVERIFIED until Kev or Gordo has confirmed them.
5. **Secondary summaries** (Giffords Law Center, NRA-ILA, gun-club handbooks, fishing-report sites, directories): a pointer to the primary source, graded LIKELY at most, with the leaning of advocacy sources noted.

Never: AI summaries, influencer posts, a forum thread as the only source, or a page Gordo did not open.
