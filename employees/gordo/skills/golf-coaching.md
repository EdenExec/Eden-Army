# Golf coaching

Gordo is Kev's practice partner and course-management caddie, not his swing teacher. Swing changes come from a lesson with a local PGA or LPGA professional, which Kev books himself (a QC item); Gordo builds the practice around what the pro said.

## Baseline (first golf session)

Ask, one or two at a time with `AskUserQuestion`:

1. Handicap Index, or typical score for 18 holes if he has no index. Does he keep a GHIN or other World Handicap System record? (Whether to post scores is his call; a handicap membership is a QC item.)
2. Rounds per month he wants in Boise, and when (early mornings, after check-out, Sunday afternoons; never the Sabbath unless Kev chooses it).
3. The miss: the shot that costs him most (driver off the tee, approach from 100 to 150, chipping, three-putts).
4. Clubs he owns and how old they are; whether he has ever been fitted.
5. Last lesson and what the pro told him.

Then measure for one week, no changes: fairways hit, greens in regulation, up-and-downs, putts per round, penalty strokes. That is the baseline table in `memory/live/README.md` (golf numbers are not sensitive; still no one else's scores).

## Weekly practice structure (about three hours, adjust to the week)

| Session | Time | What | Measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Range | 60 min | 10 min warm-up wedges; 30 min on the one thing from the last lesson, with alignment sticks; 20 min "play the course" on the range: a different club and target every ball, full pre-shot routine | Shots inside a target width per 10 |
| Short game | 45 min | Chipping ladder (land on a spot, three distances), bunker basics, 50 to 100-yard wedges (half, three-quarter, full) | Up-and-down attempts out of 10 |
| Putting | 30 min, can be at home | Gate drill (start line), 3-6-9 feet circle, lag ladder from 20, 30 and 40 feet | Makes from 3 feet in a row; lags inside 3 feet |
| On course | 9 holes when possible | Play one ball under the course-management checklist; track the baseline stats | The five stats |

Short weeks: putting at home plus one range session beats nothing. Practise the miss first: most strokes come from 100 yards and in.

## Course-management checklist (on the card or the phone)

- [ ] Before the round: wind, pin sheet if there is one, and the three holes where par is a great score
- [ ] Tee shot: pick the side of the fairway that takes trouble out of play; driver only where the miss is safe
- [ ] Approach: aim at the fat part of the green unless a wedge is in hand; club for the back of the green when unsure (most amateurs come up short)
- [ ] Trouble: get back to the fairway first; no hero shot through trees
- [ ] Short game: putt when you can, chip when you can't putt, pitch only when you must
- [ ] Putting: speed first, line second; two-putt from long range is a win
- [ ] One pre-shot routine for every shot, same tempo
- [ ] After the round: the five stats and one sentence on what to practise

## Drills library

- **Alignment-stick gate** for the club path; **towel behind the ball** for low point.
- **9-shot drill** (high, mid, low by draw, straight, fade) once the basics hold.
- **Ladder wedges:** 50, 60, 70, 80, 90 yards, five balls each, write the carry.
- **Up-and-down game:** 9 balls around one green, count saves.
- **Clock putting:** 3 feet around the hole, all 12 before moving to 4 feet.
- **Lag ladder:** three balls from 20, 30 and 40 feet, each inside the length of the putter.
