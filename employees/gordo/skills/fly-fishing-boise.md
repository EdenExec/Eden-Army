## The rules: always the stretch, never the river

IDFG writes rules by section. Before every trip, open the Fishing Planner page for the exact stretch and the 2025 to 2027 rules booklet (2nd edition, revised 2026): https://idfg.idaho.gov/rules/fish and https://idfg.idaho.gov/ifwis/fishingplanner/. General Southwest Region rule, read 15 Nov 2026 [SETTLED]: open all year unless a special rule says otherwise; trout limit 6 combined; brook trout 25; bull trout catch-and-release everywhere ("No black, put it back"); no fishing inside the posted boundaries of a weir or trap; possession is three times the daily limit.

## Reading the gauge and the weather

1. Open the USGS page for the stretch: `https://waterdata.usgs.gov/monitoring-location/<site number>/`. Read discharge (cubic feet per second) and the trend over 7 days. A sudden jump below a dam means a release change: don't wade into rising water.
2. Know the number for that water. Gordo keeps a "fishable band" per water in `memory/live/trip-log.md` built from Kev's own days (cfs, how it waded, how it fished), because a band from a forum is a guess. Until there are three logged days, mark the band "(A)".
3. Weather: the National Weather Service Boise office (https://www.weather.gov/boi/) for wind, temperature and storms. Wind over about 15 mph makes dry flies miserable; cold fronts slow fish; summer thunderstorms in the canyons are a reason to leave.
4. Reclamation and the Corps set the dam releases. Check the Bureau of Reclamation's Pacific Northwest hydromet data for Anderson Ranch, Arrowrock and Lucky Peak when a release change is rumoured (https://www.usbr.gov/pn/hydromet/, UNSURE until opened).
5. Put the reading in the plan as one line: "SF Boise 13190500: 300 cfs, steady 7 days, wadeable at the usual spots (A)".

## Etiquette

- First angler in a run has it. Ask before you step in, and never "low-hole" (step in just below someone).
- Give space: in town, a long cast's distance at least; more on the South Fork and Silver Creek.
- Drift boats have the current: step out or stand still and let them pass; they row behind you, not over your water.
- Stay off redds (clean, swept gravel): browns in fall and winter, rainbows in spring. Teach G to spot one (Atlas covered it in episode 4).
- Release well: wet hands, keep the fish in the water, barbless hooks, quick photo or none.
- On private land, permission first; at the Silver Creek Preserve, sign in and use only the designated access points.

## Safety

- Read the gauge before you leave and again at the water. Rising water below a dam is a reason to get out now.
- Never wade above the knee in fast water; shuffle, don't step; staff on the upstream side; belt cinched. If you go in: feet downstream, on your back, swim to the bank at an angle.
- Never fish or float near a diversion dam or a whitewater park feature on the town river: the recirculation kills.
- Cold water: hypothermia sets in fast. Spare dry clothes in the truck from October to May.
- Canyons (Owyhee, South Fork): rattlesnakes in warm months, no cell coverage in places, rough roads. Tell Ally the plan.
- Lightning: off the water at the first thunder.
- Anything about an injury, heat, cold or a medical question goes to Hale.

## Skills progression

| Rung | Learn | Practice rep | Done when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Casting | The pick-up and lay-down, the overhead cast, the roll cast, line management | 15 minutes on grass, three times a week; a hula hoop at 30 and 40 feet | Lays out 40 feet straight into the hoop 7 of 10 |
| 2. Nymphing | Indicator rigs, depth, the drag-free drift, mending, the hookset | The town river in winter: one run, fished properly | Lands fish on nymphs on three separate trips |
| 3. Dry flies | Reading rises, the reach cast, the drag-free drift, timing the set | BWO days in March and October; Silver Creek is the final exam | Catches a rising fish he picked out |
| 4. Streamers | Sink-tips, the strip, swinging, the 6-weight | South Fork and Owyhee in fall | Moves fish on purpose and adjusts depth and speed |
| 5. Reading water | Riffle, run, pool, tailout; seams, eddies, foam lines, pockets (Atlas episode 2 has the picture) | Before every cast: name where the fish is and why | Picks the holding lie before he fishes it |
