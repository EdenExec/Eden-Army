# A new hobby on one page

When Kev says "I want to get into <X>", Gordo answers with one page, not a lecture. The same page works for archery, elk, upland training a dog, fly tying, rafting, skiing at Bogus Basin, woodworking, anything.

## The page

| Section | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **The goal** | Kev's words, then one measurable version with a date ("land a trout on a dry fly I tied myself by 1 June") |
| **Why it fits** | Which of his weeks it fits, G's part if any, how it serves the family and his rest (his C12 Rest & Retreat spoke is his own frame; Gordo uses it lightly) |
| **The first three sessions** | Each with: where, how long, what to practise, what done looks like. Session one is cheap and low-commitment (a free class, a rental, a borrowed kit) |
| **Gear** | What he owns already (ask), what to borrow or rent first, what to buy only after session three, each with a price read today and the link. Nothing is bought: each item is a QC spend item when Kev wants it |
| **Cost** | Start-up, the first three months, a year, as a small table; the cheapest credible path first |
| **Rules and safety** | Licenses, permits, seasons, the law (with sources and grades), and the safety rules that matter most for this hobby |
| **Who to learn from** | Local clubs, a pro or a shop, a book or course, a free video series; local names labelled UNVERIFIED until checked |
| **Next three decisions** | Each with Gordo's pick and what silence does |

## How Gordo makes it

1. Ask two questions at most first (`AskUserQuestion`, tappable, Gordo's pick first): what does good look like in a year, and how many hours a week can it have?
2. Research with WebSearch and WebFetch; every fact that changes yearly gets a link and the date read.
3. Draft with assumptions marked "(A)" rather than waiting.
4. Score it against `rubrics/hobby-work.md`.
5. Save it as `projects/hobbies/<slug>/plan.md` (`org: kdtw` front matter), render with `npm run doc` when Kev wants a PDF, and give Radar one line.
6. If G is part of it, hand Atlas a topic brief through Radar so lessons line up.

## The coach's habit

End each hobby session with the next rep: one thing, how to practise it, and how Gordo will know it worked. Then ask: what does he want to get good at next?
