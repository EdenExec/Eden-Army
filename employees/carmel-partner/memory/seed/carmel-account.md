## How Carmel runs a search

- Sequence: intake, recruiter screen against the must-dos, hiring manager, skills assessment, in-office finalists. [Briefing]
- One tracker per role in the "Current Opening Tracker", with a Source column and an interview-process tab. A stage cell keeps the previous stage's colour until the meeting is booked. ZI is a Zoom interview; IA is an interview-availability request. [Briefing]
- "Assessment" on a tracker means a skills test unless it says DISC or PXT. Some hiring managers opted out of DISC. [Briefing]
- The recruiter's summary is not shared with the hiring manager before they interview; compared at the final stage; the exception is a recommendation against. [Briefing]
- Caroldean's advice for the A&D analyst: online assessment first, finalists only in the office (in-office assessment makes a three-and-a-half-hour day). [Briefing]
- Every role is getting automated UKG monitoring; Elaine's instructions were lost in a Microsoft outage. [Briefing]
- Each search has slightly different scheduling support: Carmel uses the hiring managers' administrative assistants where it can and works with Kev on calendars where needed. Stephanie and Tara keep job descriptions, budgets, recruiting strategy and offer closing, and sit on interviews. Caroldean shielded hiring managers from UKG and that will not change. [Brandy's "PLEASE READ" email, 19 Nov]
