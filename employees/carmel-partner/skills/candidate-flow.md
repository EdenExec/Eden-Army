# Candidate flow

Keep every Carmel candidate moving and informed. Silence loses people, and over Thanksgiving Kev is the only recruiter contact many of them have.

## Stages (the tracker's `candidates.stage`)

`New` (in UKG or sourced, not screened) · `Screen` (Eden or Carmel recruiter screen against the must-dos) · `HM 1` · `HM 2` · `Assessment` (skills test, DISC or PXT) · `Final` (in-office or round robin) · `Offer` · `Accepted` · `Started` · `Out` (with who decided and why). Carmel's tracker colour rule applies to you too: a stage moves only when the meeting is actually booked, not when it is requested.

## Source types and fee basis

| Source type | Fee basis (Kev's rule, call sheet 19 Nov; Brandy's agreement pending) |
|---|---|
| Eden-sourced | 20% of first-year base (6.1), retainer credit applies |
| Carmel UKG applicant or employee referral, not interviewed with Carmel before the Start Date | 6% (6.1), no retainer credit, no guarantee |
| In process at the Start Date | Only what Schedule 2 lists for that person (9.3); "the ask" until signed |
| Outside agency (for example Fluhrer & Bridges) | No Eden fee unless Carmel approves the role in writing and the agency agrees a split (7.1); capped at 24% all in (7.2) |
| Internal promotion or transfer | No fee |

The fee basis on a row is a working note for Kev, never shown in the client view.

## The shot clock

Each candidate row has `last_touch` (the last time anyone from Eden or Carmel spoke to the candidate) and `next_due`.

| Days since last touch (working days) | Colour | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 2 | Green | None |
| 3 to 4 | Amber | A touch draft for Kev (or Noah) in Drafts |
| 5 or more, or `next_due` passed | Red | Flag in the EOD and to Kev and Michael at once |

Pending offers and accepted-not-started get a touch every 3 days (Casey's rule after the October counter-offer).

## Feedback chasing

After a submittal or interview, Carmel owes feedback within 2 business days (5.3, 11.2). On day 2, draft the chase to the hiring manager (or their assistant, from the people map) for Kev or Michael. On day 3, it goes in the EOD as red.

## The client view

The tracker has a Client view toggle that hides fee basis, owner notes, comms and every other role, so Kev can one day show a hiring manager their own pipeline. It is never shared, screen-shared or exported without Kev's yes for that exact hiring manager; a share is a `publish` QC item.

## What you never do

Reject, advance or rank a candidate out yourself (10.4); contact a candidate; ask or record pay history; put a candidate name anywhere but the tracker's database.
