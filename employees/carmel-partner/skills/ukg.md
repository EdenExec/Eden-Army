# UKG: what you can and cannot do

Kev, 19 Nov 2026: "Ideally, this agent would be able to work within UKG, essentially keeping the balls in the air, calling resumes, and keeping us moving." Here is the honest version.

## The limits

- **There is no UKG connector.** You cannot log in to UKG, read it or write it.
- **Access is a person.** Carmel may grant UKG user IDs (agreement 5.2). With an ID, a person (Kev, Noah or a named Eden recruiter, each named to Carmel under 10.2) works UKG in a browser. Later, a supervised Claude in Chrome session on Kev's machine could read screens while Kev watches; that needs Kev's yes and Brandy's, and it is not set up.
- **Carmel's data stays in Carmel's systems** (10.1). UKG resumes and Carmel's trackers stay in Carmel's fileshare. You never copy them into Crelate, the repo, Eden's Drive or a Team Hub row. Carmel resumes are kept only in Carmel's shared folder.
- **No automated tool makes or substantially assists a hiring decision** (10.4; it also keeps Eden clear of New York City Local Law 144 and California's automated-decision rules). A human decides every screen-in and screen-out.

The recruiter decides who to call and who to decline. You never auto-reject and never send a decline |

| Bulk resume download | Brandy asked Carmel's UKG contact (Norell) on 19 Nov whether the bulk download is on in the New Recruiting Experience. If it lands, the files stay in Carmel's fileshare |

## Where your summaries live

A screen summary of a Carmel applicant is Carmel data: it goes in the Carmel fileshare folder for that role (by a person) or on the candidate's tracker row as a short note, never in the repo. Remember Carmel's rule: the recruiter's summary is not shared with the hiring manager before they interview, except when the recruiter recommends against a candidate.
