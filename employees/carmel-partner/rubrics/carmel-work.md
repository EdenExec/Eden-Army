# Rubric: Carmel work (an intake record, a tracker pass, a comms pass, an EOD, a candidate-flow pass)

Score each 0 (fail), 1 (partial), 2 (strong). Pass line: 16 of 20, with no zero.

1. Result first: what changed, what is late, what Kev or Michael must do, before any detail.
2. Nothing dropped: every line of the call, note or thread is a tracker change, a step, a people-map fact, a draft or a "could not place".
3. Sourced: every number and date carries its source (call sheet, agreement section, email with time, Kev's note); unchecked facts say UNVERIFIED.
4. Clock honest: each open Carmel thread has who owes the reply and since when; 1-hour reds and 2-business-day feedback reds come from this pass's data, in the sender's working hours.
5. Tracker written safely: read before write, one batch, `if_version` on every existing document, failed pins re-read, no deletes, source on every row.
6. People map learned: habits updated from what happened (reply speed, who scheduled), predictions checked and marked held or missed, scheduler mapping marked "to confirm" until seen; work facts only.
7. Sends held to the rule: the only sends are status, EOD or flags to Kev's and Michael's Eden addresses; everything to Carmel, candidates or Alan Fluhrer is a draft; nothing on the Sabbath.
8. Agreement held: Carmel data stays in Carmel's systems (10.1); no automated decision, ranking out or rejection (10.4); fee basis quoted from the call sheet or the agreement, never invented; no lead generation before signature except the Marina Shores PE.
9. Privacy held: no candidate name in the repo, a ticket or a deck; no phones, emails, pay history or ages on shared surfaces; pay expectations only; no other client named; the Denver backfill kept off.
10. Eden standard and hand-off: tables, three bullets or fewer per section, no emoji; Radar gets one line and proposed ledger rows; `now.json` updated.
