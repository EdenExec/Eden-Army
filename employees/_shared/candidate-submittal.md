# Eden's Candidate Submittal Template

Kev, 16 Nov 2026: "standardizing our submittals moving forward in favor of just matching, not selling. lets make sure all new hires are trained with this."

This is the only format for a candidate submittal to a client. It **matches** the candidate to the role's Must Do's with facts; it does not sell. No adjectives about the candidate, no "rock star", no "perfect fit", no pitch paragraph. The facts make the case.

## The template (copy exactly)

```
Subject: <Candidate Name> | <Title> | <Location>

Attached for your review is the resume and qualifications of <Candidate Name>, who is interested in the <Job Title> role in <Location>. Please see additional qualifications below:

Candidate Name:
Salary Expectations:
Role Submitted for:
Current Location:
Target Project Locations:

Job Match:
Direct experience matching the role's "Must Do's":
• <one bullet per Must Do: the Must Do it answers, then the candidate's direct experience with project, scale and years>

Career history:
<Full chronological work history whenever possible: company, title, dates, project types and sizes. Explain any move of less than 3 to 4 years in detail: why they left and what changed.>

Start Availability:
Actively Interviewing:

Interview Availability:
```

## How to fill it (matching, not selling)

- **Job Match comes from the client's Must Do's.** Pull the Must Do's from the JO in Crelate or Casey's board. Write one bullet per Must Do, in the client's order. Each bullet is evidence: the project, its size, the candidate's role and the years. If the candidate doesn't meet a Must Do, leave it out of Job Match and tell the owner recruiter and Kev before it goes; never stretch a bullet to cover a gap.
- **Career history is complete and chronological.** Every company, title and set of dates, newest first, as on the resume. Any stint under 3 to 4 years gets a plain explanation: why the candidate left. No gaps left unexplained.
- **Salary Expectations** is the candidate's stated expectation for this role (base, plus bonus if they gave one), confirmed on the screen. Never their current or past pay, and never Eden's fee.
- **Actively Interviewing** is yes or no plus how far along ("Yes, final round with one other GC"). Never name the other company.
- **Interview Availability** gives real windows: days and times, with time zone.
- **No other client is named** anywhere: not who else is seeing the candidate, not other searches. No phone numbers or personal emails in the body; the resume attachment is the formatted Eden version.
- **Plain facts, short lines.** No "rock star", no "perfect fit", no "we're excited". If a sentence sells rather than matches, cut it.

## The check before it goes

1. The subject line matches the format exactly: Name | Title | Location.
2. Every Must Do on the JO is either matched by a bullet or flagged to the owner recruiter and Kev.
3. The career history is complete, and every move under 3 to 4 years is explained.
4. No other client is named, and there's no current or past pay and no fee.
5. Kev reviews before it's submitted (Kev, 27 Oct 2026). The recruiter sends; no agent sends.

This replaces the 8 Oct 2026 overview format ("Candidate submittal · <title>", "Why he fits the seat", "Interviews"). The client-agnostic rules from 8 Oct still apply.
