2. Add the exclusions in every search: Company, Exclude: Moss, Swinerton, XL Construction, Triton, PB South, Promethean, VMG, Megawatt, Hoffman, Pence Kelly, Fortis, Carmel, Prometheus. Also exclude homebuilders (for example Taylor Morrison, Lennar) and electrical or trade subs if they show up.

5. Lists, open the list, Export (CSV). Confirm the credit type on the confirmation screen says bulk or export credits before accepting. Drop the CSV in the Leads Buckets Drive folder.
