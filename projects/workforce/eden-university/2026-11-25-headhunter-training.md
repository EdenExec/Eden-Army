## Recruiting · 4.6 Autopsy (18 min): read your own scorecard

The point: the scorecard is a mirror, not a report card. Each person reads their own card and finds the one leak. Numbers stay private: nobody reads anyone else's card out loud.

1. **Open your card (2 min).** Round Table, your page, weeks of 9 and 16 Nov side by side.
2. **Read it in order (6 min).** Kev walks the funnel once, the same order for everyone:
   - Activity: RPs a day against the 15 standard. Is the effort there?
   - Conversion: RPs to screens, screens to submittals, submittals to FTIs. Where does it narrow the most?
   - Inventory: MPCs and active JOs. Is there enough on the desk to convert?
   - The Must-Dos and Candor: where you rated yourself honestly, and where you'd rate yourself lower if Kev asked.
3. **Find the leak (5 min, pen).** Write one line: "My biggest leak is ___, because ___." Activity, conversion or inventory: pick one. (Tie to 4.5 desk economics later; today is just naming it.)
4. **One fix for next week (3 min).** One change you control, measurable on next Friday's card. "More calls" doesn't count; "15 RPs by noon Mon to Thu" does.
5. **Kev's read (2 min).** What he'll look for on every card from now on: the trend, not one week.

## Construction · 3.10 (12 min): the Director of Preconstruction

**Who they are:** runs estimating and preconstruction at a GC. Reports to the VP of Ops, GM or president. Hires chief and senior estimators, estimators, precon managers, sometimes VDC (21 Oct: precon vs estimating).

| | Director of Preconstruction |
|---|---|
| Measured on | Win rate (hit rate on bids and negotiated work), estimate accuracy against final cost, getting the job to operations with a budget that holds |
| Up at night | Losing bids on price because the numbers aren't sharp; an estimate that busts in the field and gets blamed on precon; one senior estimator leaving with the relationships |
| Wants from a recruiter | Estimators who know the trade and the sector (self-perform or not, healthcare vs multifamily), owner and design-team relationships, people who can run a GMP or design-build pursuit |
| Say | "He priced the last three hospital jobs his team won, and they came in within 2% of budget" |
| Don't say | "Great with numbers" or "detail oriented" with no win or accuracy to back it |

**Ask on intake:** "Bid work, negotiated, or both? What's the hit rate you want?" · "Which sector, and what size of job does this person price?" · "Who decides: you, or the VP of Ops?"

**Drill (2 min):** pick one estimator or precon candidate on your desk. Write one "say" line for a Director of Preconstruction: a win or an accuracy number. If you can't, that's your next screen question.
