## Job description additions (paste-ready)

**[Title TBD], Candidate and Client Success**

You are the back end of the Eden recruiting desk. The recruiters hunt; you make sure every candidate they move forward has a clean, white-glove journey and that nothing sits still.

- Keep the candidate journey moving from submittal to day 90: logistics, prep, debriefs, start-date confirmations and check-ins.
- Run set check-ins with every placed candidate (day before start, week 1, day 30, day 60, day 90), log them in Crelate, and flag any retention risk to the recruiter the same day.
- Ask every happy placed candidate who else in their network we should talk to (the Money Circle), and pass referrals and new job leads to the recruiter.
- Gatekeep candidates from our 1099 partner network: log, review against the job order, green or red light, and hand off.
- Keep the WIP honest: every active deal has a next action, an owner and a date. Flag at-risk deals before end of day.
- Support client success: confirm interview times with clients, chase feedback the recruiter is waiting on, and send the client a short check-in after each start.
- Draft submittals, interview prep sheets and follow-up emails with Claude, for the recruiter to review.
- System hygiene: if it isn't in Crelate, it didn't happen.

## The candidate journey: what Riley does at each stage

The recruiter owns the relationship and the decisions. Riley owns the logistics, the follow-through and the record.

| Stage | Riley's action | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Screen** | Partner submittals: log, check resume + job order, red-flag review, green or red light, hand off to the recruiter. Recruiter screens: make sure notes and tags are in Crelate. | Same day it arrives |
| **Submit** | Draft the submittal doc from call notes, transcript and resume for the recruiter to review. Then chase client feedback through the recruiter. | Draft before submittal; feedback chase at 48 hours |
| **Interview** | Confirm time, place and attendees with candidate and client; send the invite (once the recruiter OKs it). Make sure the recruiter's prep call is booked. | As soon as scheduled; prep 48 hours before |
| **Debrief** | Call the candidate: how it went, interest level 1 to 10, concerns, other offers in play. Log it and tell the recruiter. | Within 24 hours of the interview |
| **Close** | Help pre-close: list the candidate's motivators and risks, prep them for a counteroffer conversation with the recruiter, and track offer, acceptance and resignation dates. | Before the offer goes out |
| **Start** | Confirm the start date in writing, call the day before ("anything you need?"), and call at the end of week 1. Send the client a short "how is the first week going?" | Day before start, end of week 1 |
| **Retain** | The check-in cadence below. | Day 30 to day 90 |

Red-flag review for partner submittals (from Bran's guide): stints under 1.5 to 2 years, gaps over 3 to 6 months unexplained, flat or backwards progression, weak match to the job order's must-haves, sloppy resume. Green light goes to the recruiter with strengths and yellow flags; red light gets a warm, short no to the partner.

## Check-in cadence for placed candidates

| When | Who Riley calls | What she's listening for | The ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day before start | Candidate | Paperwork, nerves, anything missing | "Anything you need from us?" |
| End of week 1 | Candidate, then a short note to the client | Is it what they were sold? | none yet |
| Day 30 | Candidate | Transition, manager fit, workload, surprises | Money Circle: "who else should we talk to?" |
| Day 60 | Candidate; client contact if the recruiter wants | Performance, any early sign of leaving | Referrals; any new roles on the client's team |
| Day 90 | Candidate and client | Ramp done? Guarantee period clean? | Testimonial, referrals, new job orders |

After day 90 the candidate moves to Ember's warm inventory (no set Riley cadence).

Rules:
- Every call is logged in Crelate the same day with the next touch date set.
- Any retention risk (unhappy, being recruited, manager friction) goes to the recruiter the same day. Riley doesn't try to fix it herself.
- Nothing personal goes in the notes: work only (role, manager, workload, plans). No pay talk unless the recruiter asks her to.
- Ember can keep the due list: each weekday it can show Riley who is due a touch.

## Client success, kept simple

- Confirm every interview with the client contact in writing.
- Chase feedback at 48 hours (the recruiter decides how hard to push).
- After every start: a week-1 note to the client, a day-30 and day-90 check.
- Anything a client says about a new need goes to the recruiter and Casey's board the same day.
- Never one client's name in front of another client.

## Her rhythm

**Daily**
1. Morning: check Crelate and the board for anything that moved overnight; check the partner submittal inbox.
2. Work the check-ins due today (Ember's list).
3. Chase pending feedback and confirmations.
4. End of day: every active deal she touched has a next action, owner and date; flag at-risk deals to the recruiter.

**Weekly**
- Monday stand-up: attend and capture action items.
- Thursday: the WIP list is clean before the leadership meeting (she preps it; leadership runs it).
- Friday: scorecard (support version, below).
