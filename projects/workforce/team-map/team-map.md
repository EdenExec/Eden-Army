## What we can and can't predict

- **Can, now:** how to coach and talk with each person, where the team leans, and where friction is likely. Mostly 3s: driven by results and being seen to win, which fits a scoreboard culture (the Friday scorecard plays to them). The watch-outs are image over honesty when numbers slip, and burning out chasing the next number. 8s (Nick, Riley): direct, protective, want control and straight talk. A 3 leader with 8s works when the 8 gets real authority and the 3 doesn't spin.
- **Can, over time:** pair each person's type with their scorecard trend, ramp time and tenure. With under 10 people this is anecdote, not proof. Revisit at 15 to 20 people and at least two quarters of scorecards.
- **Won't:** use type to decide a hire or rank candidates. Research doesn't support type predicting job performance, and screening on it carries legal risk.
