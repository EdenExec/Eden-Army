Kev, 11 Nov 2026: track Enneagram and Working Genius for every new hire and the existing team; pay a small amount so both Kev and the person get the result; build a team map. Kev sets up Working Genius himself.

## How it runs
1. Kev buys codes at tests.enneagraminstitute.com (a 10-pack is $160). Set results to "taker and administrator." Over 20 codes needs a free Customer Account. Codes expire a year after purchase.
2. Codes arrive in a spreadsheet; Bridger hands one code per person (new hires in Week 0; existing team this month).
3. Each person takes it at tests.enneagraminstitute.com with the code; Kev downloads results.
4. Dean records on the team map only: name, Enneagram type and wing, top two Working Genius and two frustrations. No full reports in the repo or on a team surface.

## Guardrails
- Development and team fit only, after an offer is accepted. Never a pass/fail or ranking factor in hiring. (Personality tests used to screen carry legal risk; Docket to confirm the wording before we tell candidates.)
- The person chooses to share; they get their own report.
- The pre-screen and next-round invites that ask candidates for an Enneagram link: move that ask to post-offer.
