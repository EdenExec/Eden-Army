Why this matters

This is for you before it is for us. The people who grow fastest know what drives them, what they avoid, and how they act under pressure. The Enneagram is one of the best mirrors we know for that. "Know thyself" is the oldest advice in leadership for a reason.

Your motivation beats our goals for you. Nothing we set for you will outlast what you want for yourself. When we understand what moves you, we can coach you the way you need, not the way that is easiest for us.

It makes the team better. We map how we work together: where we balance each other, where friction starts, and how to talk so each person hears it.

What it isn't. It isn't pass or fail, and there is no best type. Every type has real strengths and real blind spots. Think of it as a mirror, not a box.

Before you start

  • Block 45 minutes, in one sitting. It is 144 pairs of statements, about 40 minutes.
  • Use a laptop somewhere quiet, not your phone between calls, and not at the end of a draining day.
  • Skip the free online quizzes first. Come to this one fresh.

How to answer

  1. Answer for most of your adult life, not this past month, not who you want to be, and not who your job asks you to be.
  2. Think about why you do things, not only what you do. Two people can act the same way for very different reasons.
  3. Pick the statement that is more true. If both fit, choose the one that fits better. If neither fits, choose the one that is less wrong.
  4. Go with your first honest pick. Don't overthink it.
  5. Answer as the whole you, at home and with friends too, not your work self only.
  6. Nobody is grading you. If you answer the way you think Eden wants, you get someone else's result.

Taking it

  1. Bridger sends you a test code. Each code works once.
  2. Go to tests.enneagraminstitute.com and enter the code, your name and your email.
  3. Finish in one go. Your results appear at the end and come to you by email.

After you finish

  • Read your top three types, not only the top score. The test is a starting point. You confirm your type by reading the descriptions and recognising yourself.
  • Ask someone who knows you well which description sounds most like you.
  • Bring three lines to your next check-in with Kev: what rang true, what surprised you, and one place you want to grow.

Who sees what. You get your full report, and Kev gets a copy. The team map shows only your type, your wing and your Working Genius pair. You decide whether to share your report beyond that.
