# Playbook: Recruiter (headhunter)

Status: v1, pulled from Eden University (`projects/eden-university/stand-up-your-desk/course.md`, `arc.md`) and the Friday scorecard (`employees/dean/skills/weekly-scorecard.md`). Desk: Max, Evan, Noah, Andrew, Bridger.

## 1. The role in one line
Run your own desk: source, screen, present and close executive-search placements in commercial construction. Serves clients (general contractors, specialty firms) and candidates.

## 5. Scorecard
Weekly counts from Crelate: dials, RPs (15 a day, 75 a week), MPs, JOs, resumes, screens, PTCs, submittals, FTIs, referrals, LVMs, plus the 42-box card and the Seven Pillars. The six Must-Dos are the frame.
