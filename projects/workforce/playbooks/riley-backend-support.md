## 1. The role in one line
**[Title TBD], Candidate and Client Success.** The back end of the recruiting desk: every candidate a recruiter moves forward gets a clean, white-glove journey and nothing sits still. Serves the five recruiters, their candidates and clients, and the 1099 partner network.

## 2. Job description
Paste-ready text is in the pack, section "Job description additions". Eight duties: keep the journey moving from submittal to day 90; set check-ins (day before start, week 1, day 30, 60, 90) logged in Crelate; Money Circle referral ask; partner gatekeeper; honest WIP; client success follow-through; Claude-drafted submittals and prep sheets for recruiter review; Crelate hygiene.

## 3. Workflows
| Stage | Riley's action | When |
|---|---|---|
| Screen | Partner submittals: log, check against the job order, red-flag review, green or red light, hand off | Same day |
| Submit | Draft the submittal from notes and resume (match, don't sell); chase client feedback via recruiter | Before submittal; 48 hours |
| Interview | Confirm time, place, attendees; invite once recruiter OKs; confirm prep call is booked | On scheduling; prep 48 hours before |
| Debrief | Call candidate: how it went, interest 1 to 10, concerns, other offers; log; tell recruiter | Within 24 hours |
| Close | List motivators and risks; prep for counteroffer talk with recruiter; track offer, acceptance, resignation dates | Before offer |
| Start | Confirm start in writing; call the day before and end of week 1; short note to client | Day before; week 1 |
| Retain | Check-in cadence below. **Stops at day 90.** | Day 30 to 90 |

Red-flag review: stints under 1.5 to 2 years, unexplained gaps over 3 to 6 months, flat or backwards progression, weak match to must-haves, sloppy resume. Green light: to recruiter with strengths and yellow flags. Red light: warm, short no to the partner.

## 4. Cadences
| When | Cadence |
|---|---|
| Daily | Morning: Crelate, board, partner inbox. Midday: check-ins due (Ember's list), chase feedback. End of day: every deal touched has next action, owner, date; flag at-risk deals |
| Weekly | Mon stand-up (attend, capture actions). Thu WIP list clean before leadership meeting. Fri support scorecard |
| Per placed candidate | Day before start, end of week 1, day 30 (Money Circle ask), day 60, day 90 (testimonial, referrals, new job orders). **No set check-in after day 90**; candidate goes to Ember's warm inventory |
