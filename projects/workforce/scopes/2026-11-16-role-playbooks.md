## What it is

One private artifact, one tab per role. Each role page has the same sections, so every playbook reads the same way:

1. **The role in one line** and who it serves
2. **Job description** (paste-ready)
3. **Workflows**: the stage table (what, who, when)
4. **Cadences**: daily, weekly, and any set check-ins
5. **Scorecard**: the role's Must-Dos (feeds the Round Table)
6. **First 90 days**: milestones by week
7. **Tools and who to ask**: systems and the agents that support the role
8. **Open decisions**: Kev's calls, logged with the date

Kev can comment or edit in place during the working sessions; edits save to the page's database so nothing is lost between sessions.
