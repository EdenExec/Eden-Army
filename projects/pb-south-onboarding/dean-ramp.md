# PB South Construction: Onboarding Ramp Concepts

## Executive summary

**Result:** Two cohort starts a month (1st and 3rd Monday), a hard paperwork cutoff counted back from Day 1, one 30/60/90 ramp in three role families, and a named coach for every new hire. Draft for Kev. Items marked (A) are assumptions to confirm with PB South's GM and HR/admin.

## 1. Cohort model

| Count back from start (Monday) | Must be done | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Start minus 10 days (Friday) | Offer accepted; hire enters the cohort roster (A) | Recruiter |
| Start minus 7 days | Offer letter, I-9 Section 1 and tax forms sent; background and drug test ordered (A) | HR/admin |
| Start minus 5 days | Insperity enrollment record created; role family and coach assigned | HR/admin, hiring manager |
| Start minus 3 days | Background and drug test cleared; laptop imaged; email and system accounts live | HR/admin, IT |
| Start minus 1 day (Friday) | Day 1 readiness check passed; welcome logistics confirmed (parking, PPE, 8 to 5 start, first-day location) | HR/admin |

- **Missed cutoff:** the start rolls to the next cohort, two weeks later. No half-ready Day 1. Exception only by GM sign-off, logged.
- **Cutoff clock:** the clock stops only on a green item, not a submitted one (a pending test is not cleared).
- **Benefits timing (per handbook):** insurance starts the 1st of the month after 30 days; 401k after 3 months with a 4% match; PTO usable after day 30. Review these on Day 3 so nothing surprises anyone.

**Cohort week (Day 1 to Day 5, 8 to 5)**

| Day | Together (whole cohort) | By role family |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Welcome from GM; company story and values; paperwork and system log-ins; meet your coach | Coach walk-through of the workplace and the week |
| 2 | Safety orientation; how PB South works (organization, projects, who to call) | Field: site and PPE walk. PM: project list and tools. Office: systems and close calendar |
| 3 | Benefits and Insperity walk-through (health plan, 401k, commuter, FSA); handbook confirmation | Role deep-dive with hiring manager |
| 4 | Culture session with leadership: how we communicate, how we give feedback | Shadow day with coach in the real work |
| 5 | Cohort lunch and retro (what confused you?); 30/60/90 plan read-out | Write the personal 30-day goals with the manager |

## 2. The 30/60/90 ramp

Role-agnostic spine: **30 days learn** (rules, people, systems), **60 days contribute** (do the work with a checker), **90 days own** (do it alone and improve it). Observable means someone could watch it or read a record.

| Family | Day 30: on track | Day 60: on track | Day 90: on track |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Field** (supers, assistant supers, field engineers, safety) | Completed site safety orientation and required trainings; can name the trades and the schedule's next 3 milestones; ran a daily huddle with coach watching | Leads daily logs and the weekly look-ahead with edits, not rewrites; closed first inspections or RFIs on their own scope | Runs their area of the job day to day; owns a look-ahead; no repeat safety findings in the last 30 days |
| **Project management** (PMs, APMs, PEs, project engineers, precon and estimating) | Can find any contract, submittal and RFI in the system; shadowed an owner meeting and a sub meeting; first logs filed on time | Runs a submittal or RFI cycle end to end; produces a draft cost report or bid package for review; leads one internal meeting | Owns a defined scope (project, bid or package) and reports status without prompting; one process improvement proposed |
| **Office** (accounting, admin) | Core systems access works; completed one full cycle of their main task (payables, entries, filing) under review; knows the close calendar | Runs the cycle with spot checks only; first month-end support delivered on time | Owns the recurring cycle; documents it so a backup could run it; error rate reviewed with manager |
| **Who checks** | Day 30: manager and coach, using the 30-day checklist | Day 60: manager, coach, and one peer from the role family | Day 90: manager and GM, using the 90-day checklist |

**Existing check-ins, kept as the formal record**

| Checkpoint | Carry over from PB South's checklist | Add (A) |
|---|---|---|
| 30 days | Handbook confirmed; benefits reviewed; integration and satisfaction; role understanding; early feedback; goals for 90; blockers; culture | Milestones above ticked yes/no; pulse score (section 4) |
| 60 days | none today | Short manager and coach check on milestones and blockers |
| 90 days | Yearly goals; progress; verbal feedback; leadership support; culture; resources; open forum | Milestones ticked; pulse score; stay-or-adjust note |

## 3. Coach and buddy model

- **Who:** a peer in the same role family, at least 12 months in, not the direct manager, volunteers vetted by the GM (A). One coach per two new hires at most.
- **When:** assigned at start minus 5 days; coach makes contact before Day 1; formal coaching runs 90 days, then drops to open door.

| Week | Coach owes the new hire |
|---|---|
| 1 | Meet before Day 1; host Day 1 and lunch; daily 10-minute close-out (what went well, what is confusing) |
| 2 to 4 | Weekly 30-minute 1:1; one real-work shadow session; introductions to 3 key people; read the 30-day milestones together |
| 5 to 8 | Weekly 1:1; watch one task done by the hire and give one change to make; flag blockers to the manager within 48 hours |
| 9 to 13 | Every other week 1:1; help draft the hire's own 90-day review notes; recommend one thing to improve in onboarding |

Coach records: one line per week (met yes/no, topic, blocker). That log feeds the scorecard.

## 4. Program scorecard

Definitions only. Set targets after two or three cohorts of PB South's own data (none are sourced yet).

| Metric | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Time from accept to start | Calendar days from offer accepted to Day 1, per hire; report the median and the count that rolled a cohort | Offer date, start date |
| Day 1 readiness | Share of the cohort with every Day 1 item green at start minus 1 day (paperwork, Insperity record, clear test, laptop, email, coach); report per item as well | Friday readiness check |
| 90-day retention | Hires still employed at Day 90 divided by hires who started, per cohort; add 30 and 60 as early signals | HR/admin records |
| New-hire pulse | Three questions at Day 5, 30, 60 and 90, scored 1 to 5: I know what is expected of me; I have what I need to do my job; I would recommend working here. Report the average per question and the count of responses | Short form |

## 5. Ideas to make it an Eden program

| Idea | What it is | Why it sells |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Cohort-in-a-box kit | The cutoff calendar, readiness checklist, Day 1 to 5 agendas, 30/60/90 milestone sheets and coach guide, with a client's logo and role families filled in | A contractor can run it in one hiring cycle without building anything |
| 2. Placed-hire ramp | Every Eden placement at a participating client starts in a cohort and gets the scorecard; Eden reports readiness and retention back to the client | Ties Eden's placement to 90-day results, a differentiator in placement |
| 3. Coach certification | Short Eden University module and checklist for client coaches (what they owe each week, how to give one change) | Makes the coach model repeatable as the client scales, and recurring training |
