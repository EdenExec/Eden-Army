# PB South onboarding upgrade (draft for Kev, 12 Nov 2026)

Kev with Jeff Rosser (GM): PB South's onboarding is clunky and new hires aren't in a streamlined process. Goal: a standard, cohort-based onboarding that scales, and a role-agnostic new-hire kit with the Eden touch. It could later become a program Eden sells. Nothing here has gone to PB South.

## Recommendations for Jeff (my picks)

1. **Cohort starts on the 1st and 3rd Monday** (the PENTA model). Every offer names a cohort date. A start that misses the paperwork cutoff rolls to the next cohort, two weeks later. Cutoffs, the Day 1 to 5 week and the 30/60/90 ramp by role family are in Dean's draft, `dean-ramp.md`.
2. **One owner for readiness.** Carol (or whoever owns HR) runs a readiness check the Friday before each cohort, covering Insperity, I-9, laptop, email, access and coach. Nobody arrives to an empty desk.
3. **A coach for every hire**, from the same role family, named 5 days before the start. Add a 60-day check to their existing 30/90.
4. **One kit for every hire, not one deck per person:** the role-agnostic deck below, plus a one-page "your role and your coach" insert the manager fills in.
5. **Three fixes to make now** in their current materials:
    - the Wi-Fi password and door codes are printed in the deck, so give them in person;
    - the senior team slides carry a wrong email address (another company's);
    - the dress code paragraph says "Promethean", not PB South.

## Flags

- **The handbook (10 Mar 2025)** cites New York State and NYC sick-leave law for a Florida company. Worth a gentle question to Carol, or a look from Docket, before we paraphrase it.
- **The 2026 holiday list** in their deck follows the NYSE calendar, including Good Friday, while the handbook lists a slightly different set. Carol confirms which is right.
- **The logo** in the deck is a small raster. Ask Jeff for the vector logo and any brand colours or fonts. v1 uses a navy drawn from their deck, and Arial so it opens cleanly anywhere.
- **Headshots** of the leadership team are baked into slide images. Ask for the original photos.

## Selling it later (Eden program)

"Cohort-in-a-box": the deck template, ramp, coach guide, readiness checklist and scorecard, rebranded per client and set up in a week. PB South is the pilot case study, with Jeff's yes. Pricing goes through Tally and terms through Docket when Kev wants to package it.
