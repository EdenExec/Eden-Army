# Welcome to PB South · Presenter guide

For whoever runs the cohort welcome. It goes with `welcome-to-pb-south-v3.pptx`: 11 slides, about 45 minutes, and every slide has speaker notes. Kev, 12 Nov 2026: the role and coach conversation is the presenter's to deliver in person, not a slide.

## Before the session (the Friday before the cohort)

- Confirm each new hire's coach and first project or desk.
- Have the Employee Handbook and acknowledgement printed for each person.
- Have Wi-Fi details and building access ready to hand over in person. They never go in the deck.

## Your role and your coach (cover on slide 6, one person at a time)

| Cover | Say it plainly |
| --- | --- |
| Title | "You're joining as [title]." |
| What they own | One line: what this role is accountable for, and why it matters to the project |
| Reports to | Name and title, and how often they'll meet |
| First project or desk | Where they'll be working in week one |
| Coach | Name, role and how to reach them. Coaches are in the same role family and named 5 days before the start |
| What the coach does | Week 1 walkthrough; weekly questions, feedback and introductions; the 30, 60 and 90 day check-ins |
| 30-day check-in | Put the date in the calendar before they leave the room |

## Running order

| Slides | Minutes | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 to 4 | 10 | Welcome, who we are, leadership (bring a leader in for 5 minutes if you can) |
| 5 to 6 | 15 | The first week, then role and coach per person, then 30/60/90 |
| 7 to 9 | 10 | Pay and benefits, holidays, how we work. The handbook and Insperity govern; don't quote beyond the slides |
| 10 to 11 | 10 | Day 1 checklist done live, then close |
