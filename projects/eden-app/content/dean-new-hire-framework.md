# New-hire artifact framework (Dean, draft 19 Nov 2026)

One framework; each new hire gets their own private artifact from it. Work-only. Nothing is sent to a hire until Kev says yes. Names and roles only.

## The framework (every hire)

| Section | What it holds | Checker |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome | Name, role, start date, manager, the Seven Pillars | Kev |
| Week 1 | Accounts live, Day 1 checklist, handbook read, first shadowed session. Gate: desk live | Nick (accounts), manager |
| Day 30 | Daily rhythm running, first scorecard, role-specific core skill. Gate: first desk running | Manager |
| Day 60 | Owning a piece of work end to end, one audit of own work. Gate review in person | Kev or Nick |
| Day 90 | Full role, own plan for next quarter. Gate: full desk, sign-off in person | Kev |
| Training | Assigned Eden University track with progress; Wednesday Headhunter Training; Recruiter Kit where relevant | Dean tracks |
| Scorecard | The role's weekly numbers, this week, last week, four-week average. Discussed in person, never by invite | Nick |
| Shadowing | Schedule of sessions to watch and be watched: who, what skill, date, done | Manager |

Rules: Day 1 to 90 outcomes are testable by a person. Sign-offs are recorded as a date only. Scorecards of interns are visible to Bridger; recruiter scorecards to Kev and Nick.
