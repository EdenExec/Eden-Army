# Stream Deck +, design direction v2

**My recommendation: a warm black panel of eight quiet keys, where words and simple shapes do the work.** The top row is the four things you reach for. The bottom row puts your four desktops one press away. The strip shows your next meeting and goes cream 15 minutes out, the same as the Day Board on the iPad beside it.

## Simple and on brand

- **One shape per key, at most two strokes.** A ring for Radar, a framed square for Command, two lines for a note, an arrow for Join. No pictures of objects, no detail.
- **Type leads.** The word under each key is larger than in v1 (Montserrat semi-bold, capitals, wide spacing). Desktop keys carry no icon at all: a large numeral and a name.
- **Thin, even lines.** 4 px strokes with round ends, drawn small in the top half of the key, with plenty of dark space around them.

## Dark keys, final

Dark keys, because in a dark room under a warm lamp a cream panel would glow at you all day. Dark also makes the one cream key, the thing that matters now, impossible to miss.

## What each state looks like

| State | Look | Means |
|---|---|---|
| Ready | Charcoal key, cream mark, cream word | Press any time |
| Now | The whole key turns cream | Live: meeting soon, mic on, camera on, current desktop |
| Waiting | One brass dot with a count and a brass line | Radar or the board has something for you |
| Off | Fades into the panel | Not useful right now |
| Rest | Dim warm grey | Sabbath |

Brass is the only colour on the deck, and it only ever means "something is waiting for you".

## The strip and dials

- **The strip is for looking, the keys are for doing.** The left half is the next meeting with a countdown. The right half shows the focus timer and volume.
- **One job per dial, always the same:** DAY, DESKTOP, FOCUS, VOLUME, left to right. The name of each dial sits on the strip above it, so you never have to remember.
- **Your hand learns the dials once.** Zoom and the Sabbath change the keys, never what a dial does.

## Five rules

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| One shape and one word per key | Draw objects or add detail |
| Cream for "now" | Use red, green or blue for anything |
| Brass only for "waiting for you" | Use brass as decoration |
| Hold to Leave a call | Let one press end a meeting |
| Leave a key blank if nothing earns it | Fill a key because it is there |

Strip size is treated as about 800 x 100 px. Check it on the device before final art.
