# Stream Deck +, layout v3

**Top row: reach for it. Bottom row: go somewhere. Strip: see what's next. Dials: set it.** The deck has three pages, and it switches between them on its own.

## Home page

| | Key 1 | Key 2 | Key 3 | Key 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Top** | RADAR (open Radar's chat) | COMMAND (Command Center page) | FLOW (start Wispr Flow dictation) | JOIN (next meeting) |
| **Bottom** | 1 AGENDA (Calendar and Eden Daily split) | 2 CLAUDE (main desktop, Claude and Radar) | 3 LINKEDIN (LinkedIn and browser) | 4 MUSIC (Apple Music) |

| Key | Why it sits there |
|---|---|
| Radar | Your one key a day, so it gets the top-left corner, the first place your eye and hand go. It takes the brass dot when Radar has replied. |
| Command | Your other daily key, right next to Radar. |
| Flow | Dictation between calls, so it stays in the top row and in reach. Five bars, never confused with the call's Mic. |
| Join | Far right, away from Radar. Faded until 15 minutes out, then it turns cream and shows the time. One press joins. |
| Desktops 1 to 4 | Laid out in the same order as the Mac desktops, so key 1 is desktop 1. The desktop on screen now shows cream. Agenda is first because it is where the day starts. |

## Zoom page (swaps in when Zoom is in front, swaps back when it isn't)

| | Key 1 | Key 2 | Key 3 | Key 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Top** | MIC (cream when live, MUTED when off) | CAMERA (cream when on, CAM OFF when off) | SHARE (screen share) | BREAKOUT (breakout rooms) |
| **Bottom** | FLOW | RADAR | blank | LEAVE (press and hold) |

- **Mic and camera sit top left, the same spot as Radar on home.** In a call, they are the keys you need most.
- **Cream means the room can hear or see you.** The state is clear without reading the word.
- **Leave sits bottom right, behind a blank, and only works on press and hold.** One slip never ends a call.

## Sabbath page (Friday sunset to Saturday sunset)

| | Key 1 | Key 2 | Key 3 | Key 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Top** | REST (until sundown) | blank | blank | blank |
| **Bottom** | blank | blank | blank | 4 MUSIC |

- **No work keys, and no Radar, Command, Join or desktops 1 to 3.** The panel goes almost dark.
- **Music and volume stay awake** for the family.

## Strip and dials

| Dial | Strip above it | Turn | Press |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 DAY | Next meeting: title, countdown, progress line (spans dials 1 and 2) | Day Board back or forward a day | Back to today |
| 2 DESKTOP | (part of the meeting area) | Step through desktops | Mission Control |
| 3 FOCUS | Minutes set, then the countdown | Set minutes | Start or stop |
| 4 VOLUME | Level and a thin bar | Volume | Mute or unmute the Mac |

- **I chose the next meeting for the strip because it is the one thing worth seeing all day.** Its half of the strip turns cream 15 minutes out, matching the Day Board and the Join key.
- **I left the day's progress and the time to Sabbath off on workdays: they add noise.** After the last meeting on Friday, the meeting area counts down to the Sabbath. In the Sabbath it counts to sundown Saturday.
- **The desktop keys and the desktop dial do different jobs.** The keys jump straight to a named desktop. The dial is for browsing, and pressing it opens Mission Control to see them all. DAY sits under the meeting because both are about the schedule.

Strip measurements are approximate (about 800 x 100 px, four 200 px segments). Confirm them on the device.
