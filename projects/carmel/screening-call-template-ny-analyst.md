# Screening-Call Template: NY Analyst Roles

Carmel Partners & Eden Partner Group. Draft for Michael Duggan and Kev, 20 Nov 2026. Built from Caroldean Ross's screening SOP (Kev's call notes, 19 Nov 2026). Share the final version with the hiring manager as the common template.

**Before the call.** Read the resume from UKG (it stays in Carmel's folder). Check the UKG screening answers first: location (out-of-state is a decline), comp ask against the range, and the written answers. Do not ask for, record or repeat current or past pay (California and New York law).

## 1. Open (2 minutes)

- Introduce Eden as Carmel's recruiting team for this search. Confirm the role, New York, and that the call takes about 20 minutes.
- Ask whether a notetaker is acceptable only after Kev confirms with Brandy that it raises no compliance issue.

## 2. Behavioral and skills questions (6 to 8)

Generate the final set by putting the job description into Claude and asking for 6 to 8 behavioral and skills questions for this role; keep the set identical across candidates for the same role. Starting set for a FP&A or Asset Management analyst:

1. Walk me through the most complex financial model you built. What did it answer, and what did you get wrong first?
2. Tell me about a time a number in your work was wrong after someone relied on it. What did you do?
3. How do you build or review a budget against actuals? What do you do when a line is far off?
4. Describe a deadline where you had too much to do. How did you decide what to drop?
5. Tell me about working with a senior person who disagreed with your analysis.
6. Which Excel or modeling techniques do you use weekly, and where are you weakest?
7. What did you learn from your last role that you would carry into multifamily real estate?
8. Why this role at Carmel, and why now?

## 3. Disqualifying questions (ask early, end the call if not met)

- Hands-on experience with Yardi: which modules, how recently, and in what capacity?
- Authorized to work and based in or willing to relocate to the role's state, at their own choice and cost? (Out-of-state applicants are declined unless Kev says otherwise.)
- Any other must-have from the hiring manager's intake (add here per role).

## 4. Compensation

- Ask the bonus structure in their current or most recent role, and when bonuses were paid out. This is about structure and timing only.
- Ask for pay expectations: "What base salary and total compensation are you looking for in this role?"
- Never ask what they earn or earned. If they volunteer it, do not write it down.

## 5. Close

- Ask for other processes and timing, notice period, and in-person availability in New York.
- Explain next steps in plain words: Eden screens, Carmel's hiring manager reviews, then an online assessment, then finalists in the office. Do not promise a timeline Carmel has not confirmed.

## 6. After the call (same day)

- Record on the Carmel Tracker: stage, a 3-line summary, pay expectation if stated, next step and date. No phone, email, age or pay history.
- Recommend one of: top five (send to hiring manager), hold, or decline. A person decides; no score or tool decides. Hiring managers expect the top five, not 25 screens.
- Use only the UKG dispositions "decline" and "send to hiring manager". Others trigger candidate emails or invites. Declines send an automated "Carmel HR" email.

Review before it goes to the client: read every field, pay line and name.
