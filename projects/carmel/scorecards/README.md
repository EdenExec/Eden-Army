## How a filled scorecard gets back to the hiring authority (proposal)

- Kev fills a copy per candidate during the screen (the fill-in page, or a copy of the role file), then marks Top five, Hold or Decline. A person decides.
- Each copy is saved in one Drive folder per role in Eden's Drive, named by role and date, with the candidate name only in the file name and inside the file, never in this repo.
- Until Carmel gives Eden access to the fileshare and a Carmel address, Kev sends the top-five scorecards to the hiring authority through Stephanie Effros (she fronts hiring-authority contact until early December).
- Once Kev has a Carmel login, the same file goes into the role's hiring-manager folder on the Carmel fileshare, next to their tracker, and stays there (agreement 10.1: Carmel data stays in Carmel systems; scorecards about Carmel applicants belong there).
- The role template itself goes to each hiring authority first, with one question: "are we covering the right things?" Their edits become v2.
