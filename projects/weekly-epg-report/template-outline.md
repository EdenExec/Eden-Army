# Monday Morning EPG Report: template outline

For Marlowe (look) and Q (artifact template). Cream and ink, Montserrat, hairline rules, one page per section, data in tables. Artifact routes on hash: `#glance`, `#pipeline`, `#cash`, `#deals`, `#agenda`. PDF is the same five pages.

## Page 1: The 75k view (#glance)

- Masthead: EDEN PARTNER GROUP, *Monday Morning EPG Report*, week of (date), built at (time MT), source freshness line.
- Six tiles, one number each with a week-on-week arrow: open JOs; candidates past FTI; offers out; funnel week total (Dial, RP, Resume, PTC, FTI as one row); cash in AR (gated or NOT GATED); bills to sign this week (count).
- Three lines: what moves money this week; what needs Kev's yes; any surprise in the next 6 weeks.

## Page 2: WIP and pipeline (#pipeline)

- Funnel table by recruiter: Dial, RP, Resume, PTC, FTI, last week vs target (Dean).
- JO board summary by status and owner; thin depth charts flagged (Casey).
- Source line: Friday Pipeline Review refresh time.

## Page 3: Cash, invoices, bills (#cash)

- AR aging buckets (current, 30, 60, 90+), counts and totals, gate state per row.
- AP due this week: payee type and due date, with a sign-off column for Kev.
- Cash forecast, 4 weeks, one line. Every figure carries GATED or NOT GATED.

## Page 4: Deals, candidates, pitches (#deals)

- Offers out and second-phase interviews to schedule (deal desk): stage, client (or "a client (discreet)"), days since touch, next step.
- Big candidates in process (Casey) and FTIs to set; no contact details, no pay.
- Pitches out and new leads (Scout, Crelate); MPCs to take to market (Ember). UNVERIFIED where a source is missing.

## Page 5: JO meeting agenda (#agenda)

- JOs: new, active, on hold, killed, filled, each with owner (Casey).
- Critical numbers and expectations from the Friday scorecards (Dean).
- 6-week look ahead (Hawk, Jenny): PTO, events and trips, closures and holidays, marketable dates, by week. Then a dot-grid notes block for the meeting.
