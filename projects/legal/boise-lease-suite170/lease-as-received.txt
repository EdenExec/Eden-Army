Commercial Lease Agreement
This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made and effective 01/01/2027 by and between Wang – Zhang, LLC. ("Landlord") and Eden Partner Group, LLC ("Tenant"). 
Landlord is the owner of land and improvements commonly known as 3320 N. Milwaukee St., Boise Idaho 83704, Suite #170, consisting of approximately 1,403 rentable square feet, as shown on the attached Exhibit “A” (“Leased Premises”). 
Landlord desires to lease the Leased Premises to Tenant, and Tenant desires to lease the Leased Premises from Landlord for the terms, covenants, conditions and provisions set forth herein. 
Now, Therefore, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:
1. Term. Landlord hereby leases the premises above for a term of Thirty-Eight (38) months with two (2) months abated rent, Possession Date to be January 1, 2027, Rent Commencement date to be March 1, 2027 and ending thirty-eight (38) months from the possession date. Tenant shall be responsible for payment of the lease for this full term. There are no early termination provisions. 
2. Possession. Tenant shall have possession of the Lease Premises upon lease execution, delivery of first month’s rent, security deposit and certificate of insurance. 
3. Rent. Tenant shall pay to Landlord a monthly lease payment of $3,039.83 (Year 1), $$3,131.03 (Year 2), and $3,224.96 (Year 3) per month. This amount shall be due and payable on the 1st of each month. In the event that the 1st of the month falls on a weekend or a state or national holiday, the payment shall be due on the following business day. All rental payments shall made to the property owner at:  Ying Wang, at:  2425 Milwaukee, Boise, ID, 83704-5736. Tenant shall be charged $35.00 for each check that is returned. If any or all the rent is not received by the 5th day of the month, a charge of $10 per day will be charged as late fees until full rental payment is received. Rent shall have 3% annual increases beginning year 2. Tenant is granted One (1) Three (3) year option to extend the Lease upon the same terms & conditions as the initial term. Rent during the option period shall be at a 3% increase over the final year of the initial term. Tenant shall provide Landlord with not less than four (4) months written notice to extend prior to the expiration of the term. Tenant shall pay for their own internet service. 
4. Security Deposit: Upon mutual lease execution, Tenant shall pay to Landlord a deposit in the amount of $3,039.83. Upon termination of this lease, these funds shall be used to make any necessary repairs to return the premises to the condition it was in at the time of tenant’s original occupancy, reasonable wear & tear excluded. Any remaining deposit funds shall be returned to the Tenant. Deposit funds shall not be used by tenant for last month’s rent. 
5. Parking. 
Green area: Customer parking only. No tenant or employee parking. 
Yellow area: Tenant and employee parking.
Red area: No parking. These parking spots belong to the owners of the adjacent building.
6. Use. Use of Premises: Staffing firm for construction companies. There shall be no residential use of the property. Tenant shall not use the Premises for storing, manufacturing or selling explosives, flammables or other inherently dangerous substances. Tenant shall maintain reasonable noise levels and shall not engage in any activity which disrupts the other occupants of the building. The tenant shall not make any alterations, additions or improvements to the premises without the prior written consent of the landlord. 
7. Sublease and Assignment. Tenant shall not sublease all or any part of the Leased Premises, or assign this Lease in whole or in part without Landlord's written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld or delayed. 
8. Included Services. The monthly lease payment includes power, gas, sewer, water and common area maintenance. Tenants are responsible for snow removal. 
9. Entry. Landlord shall have the right to enter upon the Leased Premises with not less than 24 hours’ notice to Tenant, except in the case of an emergency, to inspect the same, provided Landlord shall not thereby unreasonably interfere with Tenant's business on the Leased Premises. 
10. The Tenant shall purchase at his own expense public liability insurance with personal injury limits of at least $1.000.000.00 for injury to one person and $2.000.000.00 for any one accident and a limit of at least $2.000.000.00 for damage to the property and shall provide satisfactory evidence thereof to the landlord and shall continue same in force and effect throughout the lease term hereof. 
11.The Tenant shall not permit or commit waste to the premises. 
12. The Tenant shall comply with all rules, regulations, ordinances code and laws of all governmental authorities having jurisdiction over the premises. 
13. Front Door Policy. Every Tenant is supplied with a key to the front door, and should lock the door behind Tenant before leaving the building. If Tenant fails to lock the front door and a break in should occur, said Tenant will be held responsible for any stolen items and/or any damage incurred to the property. 
14. At the end of the term of this lease, the Tenant shall surrender and deliver up the premises in the same condition (subject to any additions, alterations or improvements, if any) as presently exists, reasonable wear and tear excluded. 
15. The Tenant acknowledges that the Landlord is not liable for any injuries or property damage sustained by the Tenant or their guests in the parking lot, regardless of the cause, including weather conditions or natural disasters. The Tenant is responsible for maintaining their own liability insurance and assumes all responsibility for any claims or damages.
