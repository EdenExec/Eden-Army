# Eden University · Training plan overview

DRAFT for the planning session, Wed 11 Nov 2026 · Kev, Nick, Bridger · edit freely

## Why we're doing this

Kev, 9 Nov: "This will be crucial as we continue to scale. Our hiring classes should be done in bunches… I want to ensure I'm not repeating myself, am bringing in fresh material and I am taking training seriously."

- **One curriculum, two rooms.** Kev trains from Boise. Vancouver keeps growing with continued education, and the next class is trained with purpose.
- **Operational excellence first.** Nick drives it. Bridger comes in behind Nick and runs the day to day for the hiring team.
- **Built to scale.** Every level is a track people move through in Claude, and the agents direct the interns' daily work.

## The arc: six levels

| Level | Name | Who | Span | Outcome | Signs off |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Recruiter GPS | Every new hire, week 0 | 2 days | Knows where they're going and where they stand | Kev |
| 1 | Vision and 10-5-3-1 | Every new hire; everyone yearly | 1 week | A written 10-5-3-1 Kev has read | Kev |
| 2 | Operationally excellent at Eden | New class, weeks 1 to 4 | 4 weeks | Desk live, logging right, scorecard on time | Nick |
| 3 | Headhunter and niche expert | Months 1 to 6 | 5 months | Fills searches in their niche; talks like an insider | Nick |
| 4 | 201 · CEO of your desk | Months 6 to 18 | Year 1 | Owns searches end to end; builds their own book | Kev |
| 5 | Pod leader | Year 2 on, by invitation | Ongoing | Runs a pod: scorecards, coaching, QC, lead gen, commission and payroll process | Kev |

Nobody skips a level. Veterans enter at their real level. GPS and 10-5-3-1 already exist in Drive; Vancouver did them last year and updates them this November.

## Chapters worth discussing

- **3.5 Niche deep-dives:** one role family per session (precon vs estimating, VDC, superintendents, MEP, project executives, safety).
- **3.10 Who's buying:** the hiring authorities, one per session. Covers the GM, VP of Operations, VP of Field Operations, Project Executive, Director of Precon, CFO or owner and Talent Acquisition manager: what each is measured on, what keeps them up at night, what they want from a recruiter.
- **4.8 to 4.11 The client side:** selling above the line, emotional intelligence, servicing clients, and building deep relationships.
- **5.1 to 5.7 Pod leader:** leading a pod, coaching, QC, lead-gen operations, commission and payroll process (process only), running a hiring class, account leadership.
- **The session log:** every Wednesday is logged by chapter code as new, a deeper pass or a refresher, so nothing repeats by accident. Dean sends Wednesday's chapters on Tuesday morning and a touch-up before the session.

## Hiring classes in bunches

- **Size and timing:** two to five hires start the same Monday, at most one class every 6 to 8 weeks.
- **Week 0, Levels 0 and 1:** with Kev in Boise.
- **Weeks 1 to 4, Level 2:** self-serve in Claude. Nick leads; Bridger runs the day to day.
- **From month 2:** they join Wednesday Headhunter Training, plus their niche chapters.
- **Interns:** they run on their own tracks (below), with the same Week 0.

## The intern pathways

Every intern is hourly, W-2, and paid for every hour including training. Each is in person in Boise (comms is hybrid), for a semester with a fixed end date, and reports to Bridger day to day.

| Pathway | Title | What the job is | Course | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Operations Intern | Executive and admin support for Kev, Nick and Bridger: scheduling, travel options, follow-ups, onboarding trackers, lead-list checks. Never sees screening results or evaluations | 28 modules, 12 weeks | Built |
| B | Sales Intern (Jr. Headhunter) | Half research, half phone on live construction searches, with a senior recruiter listening | 27 modules, 12 weeks | Built |
| C | Client Success | Recruiter support on live searches | Not built yet | Gap |
| D1 | Marketing Intern | Graphics, decks and brand pieces in the Eden standard; never posts | 23 modules, 8 weeks | Built |
| D2 | Brand and Communications Intern | LinkedIn scheduling, capture and video on Jenny's weekly plan | 25 modules, 8 weeks | Built |

### What Bridger holds every intern to

| Standard | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Every hour on the clock | Clocked in before a module or task, out after; Bridger approves time weekly |
| Nothing leaves without an Approved mark | Interns draft; a leader approves; then it goes. They never send, post, book or spend on their own |
| Their own login, always | No shared passwords; no one works in Kev's or anyone else's account |
| Confidential by default | Clients and candidates stay private; no pay, health, family or age in any note |
| Proof for every module | A screenshot or a line in their Proof Sheet, checked within the week |
| Check-ins | Week 1, week 4, week 8 and the end review; leaders write and own every evaluation |

## How the interns take direction from our agents

The agents are being trained to be world class in their own roles. Each intern works under the agent whose role matches theirs: the agent sets the work, the intern does the human part, and a person approves anything that leaves Eden.

| Pathway | Directed by | What the agent hands them each day | Human approval |
|---|---|---|---|
| A · Operations | Radar (chief of staff); Pam for project trackers; Dean for onboarding trackers | Request-log items as tickets, with the draft half done: invite drafts, travel options, tracker updates | Bridger; Kev or Nick for anything sent |
| B · Sales | Scout (lead lists with LinkedIn links); Ember (warm candidates due a touch); Casey (job orders and depth charts) | Call lists for the day's searches, touch reminders, the brief for each search | Senior recruiter and Bridger |
| C · Client Success (to build) | Casey | Board updates, client follow-up drafts, review packs | Kev |
| D1 · Marketing | Jenny (briefs); Marlowe (design check) | The week's design briefs and the brand standard | Jenny's check, then Kev to publish |
| D2 · Comms | Jenny (weekly content plan) | Post drafts, shot lists, the schedule | Kev approves every post |

**The loop, every day:** the agent opens a ticket on the Command Center, the intern picks it up and does the work in their own account, and the agent checks the quality. Bridger approves the intern's part, and Kev or Nick approves anything outward. Agents never send for an intern, and interns never approve for an agent.
