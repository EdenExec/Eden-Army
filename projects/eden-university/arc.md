## Hiring classes, in bunches

- **One class at a time.** Two to five hires start on the same Monday. (A) No more than one class every 6 to 8 weeks, so Bridger and Nick aren't onboarding non-stop.
- **Week 0 (Levels 0 and 1) is with Kev in Boise,** in person, or on Zoom for remote hires: GPS, vision, 10-5-3-1.
- **Weeks 1 to 4 (Level 2) are self-serve in Claude,** with Nick driving operational excellence and Bridger running the day to day as the hiring team's operations lead: modules, proofs, sign-offs. This is the stand-up-your-desk track, already built.
- **From month 2 (Level 3) they join Wednesday Headhunter Training** with everyone, plus one niche chapter a week on their own desk.
- Interns follow their own tracks (intern-ops, intern-sales, intern-marketing, intern-comms), with the same Level 0 and 1 week.

## The Claude system (with Q)

The goal is that each person moves through their level in Claude, at their own pace, without Kev repeating himself.

1. **One track per level.** Each level becomes a track in the same `track.json` shape the five existing tracks use, so one page and one engine run all of them.
2. **The turret engine** (scoped 5 Nov) reads a roster row (name, level, start date, class lead), works out what's due each week and opens tickets for the class lead: sign-offs, behind, end of level.
3. **"Eden University in Claude":** a shared Claude project (or skill) that knows the person's level and next chapter. It walks them through it, asks the check questions, and records the proof. Read-only on Crelate. Never stores pay, health or family.
4. **The session log feeds it.** When a Wednesday session covers a chapter, the chapter's page gets that session's notes, so a new hire catches up from the log, not from Kev.

**Split of work:** Dean writes the chapters and keeps the log. Q builds the engine and the Claude project. Nick drives operational excellence; Bridger runs the day to day under him. Kev teaches Wednesdays and signs off Levels 0, 1, 4 and 5.
