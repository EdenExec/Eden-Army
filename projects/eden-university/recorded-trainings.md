# Recorded trainings: Eden 101 first, then the Eden U library

Kev, 24 Nov 2026: "we should do a training with the new hires where i go thru this [Eden 101: How to Headhunt]. we record and have this training filed away for all future onboardings. Same thing with everything in the Eden U library in google drive. as we roll out the trainings we record and upload to Dean to do the future training mods."

Owner: Dean. Kev teaches and records; Dean turns every recording into a module. Nothing is shared with a new hire, and no folder, invite or Zoom link is made, without Kev's yes.

## How it works (one loop per training)

1. **Kev teaches it live** to whoever is new, on Zoom with cloud recording on (Plaud in the room as backup).
2. **Recording lands in one place:** a Drive folder `Eden University / Recorded Trainings`, one subfolder per training (`101-01 Eden 101 Part 1`, and so on). Zoom cloud recordings Dean can also read directly, so a drop in Drive is only needed for Plaud or a local file.
3. **Dean builds the module within 2 working days:**
   - chapter list with timestamps, so a new hire can jump to "BATNA" without watching 50 minutes
   - a one-page summary in Kev's words (pulled from the transcript, not rewritten)
   - 5 to 8 check questions and one drill to do on the desk
   - links to the source doc in the library and the arc chapter code
   - a "what changed since the deck" note wherever Kev said something the slides don't
4. **Kev approves the module** (one read, 10 minutes). Then it goes into the new-hire ramp and the Hiring Punch List's ramp row.
5. **Future hires** watch the recording plus module in week 1 and bring the check questions to their first 1:1. Kev re-records only when the content changes.
