## Outcome

You can write a client submittal on Eden's template that matches a candidate to the role's Must Do's with facts, and that passes the five-point check without a single selling adjective.

## Why it matters

Kev, 16 Nov 2026: "standardizing our submittals moving forward in favor of just matching, not selling." Clients read dozens of pitches; they trust evidence.

- **Must-Do 6 (activity / inventory / skill):** the submittal is the skill. The facts make the case.
- **Pillars:** 05 Transparency (every gap flagged, none stretched), 07 Execution (prepared, organized, follow through).
- **Not negotiable:** Kev reviews every submittal before it goes (Kev, 27 Oct 2026). The recruiter sends.

## The rule in four lines

1. Pull the client's Must Do's from the JO in Crelate or Casey's board. One bullet per Must Do, in the client's order.
2. Each bullet is evidence: project, size, the candidate's role, years. No adjectives.
3. Full chronological history. Any move under 3 to 4 years is explained plainly.
4. A Must Do the candidate does not meet is flagged to the owner recruiter and Kev. It is never stretched to fit.

The template itself lives in `employees/_shared/candidate-submittal.md`. Copy it exactly; do not retype it from memory.

## Selling versus matching

| Selling (cut it) | Matching (write it) |
|---|---|
| Rock star super who crushes schedules | Delivered a 32-story, $180M residential tower 3 weeks ahead of schedule as Superintendent (2021 to 2024) |
| Perfect fit for your tilt-up work | Must Do "tilt-up industrial": Superintendent on four tilt-up distribution buildings, 400K to 1.1M SF, 2017 to 2023 |
| Great with owners and trades | Must Do "owner-facing weekly meetings": ran the weekly OAC meeting on a $90M hospital addition, 2022 to 2025 |
| Loyal, not a job hopper | Left in 2023 when the project closed out; the company had no next project in the region (see Career history) |
| We're excited to introduce | Attached for your review is the resume and qualifications of... |

Test for any sentence: could the client check it against the resume? If not, cut it or replace it with the fact behind it.

Everything below is made up: the person, employers, projects and numbers. Pay is shown as a placeholder, as in real work it is the candidate's stated expectation only.

**Why the short move reads clean:** the 2023 to 2025 stint is under 3 years, and the history says why in one plain sentence (project closeout, no next project). The reader does not have to ask. The other company is not named.

## The drill (this week, 90 to 120 minutes)

Write three practice submittals on the template.

1. Pick three open JOs from Crelate (or Casey's board). Copy each JO's Must Do's into a draft. Do not copy client names into any file you save in Eden University.
2. Pick a practice candidate for each from resumes in your own practice folder (04 Autopsies and notes). Use the resume's facts only. Leave contact details out of the draft entirely.
3. Write the Job Match bullets, one per Must Do. If a Must Do is not met, write "FLAG: not met, owner recruiter and Kev told" beside it, in your draft only.
4. Fill the career history in full; explain every move under 3 to 4 years.
5. Run the five-point check below on each. Fix what fails.

Interns: you do not send submittals. You write the drill so you can read a recruiter's hand-off and submittal with understanding, and Bridger reviews your drafts.
