## Overview

Eden RPO puts a dedicated recruiting team inside Carmel's hiring, always engaged, with a focus on construction and real-estate talent. When a seat opens, the team is already moving.

## How it works

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Intake | One call per role: the must-haves, the team, the timeline. Eden writes the role's scorecard and Carmel approves it. |
| Depth chart | A live bench of named candidates for each seat, kept current so a vacancy starts with a shortlist. |
| Weekly pipeline | A standing weekly review of every open role: who is in process, what is stuck, what Eden needs from Carmel. |
| Submittals | Each candidate arrives with the Eden scorecard, scored against the role's must-haves, with evidence from our screen. |
| Reporting | Every open role visible in one report, with stage counts and next steps. [confirm: report format and cadence] |
