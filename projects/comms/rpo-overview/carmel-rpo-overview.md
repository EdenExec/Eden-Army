## Overview

Eden RPO gives Carmel a dedicated recruiter, always engaged, focused on construction and real-estate talent. Eden leadership runs quality control, so Carmel deals with one recruiting partner.

## How it works

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| Intake and depth chart | One call per role to set the must-haves; a live bench of named candidates for each seat. |
| Weekly cadence | A weekly talent review and WIP sync on every open role, plus recurring interview blocks on leadership calendars for speed-to-hire. |
| Submittals | Each candidate arrives with the Eden scorecard, scored against the role's must-haves. |
| Referrals and agencies | Eden screens employee referrals, with interview notes for the weekly staffing call, and manages any outside agencies on Carmel's behalf. |
